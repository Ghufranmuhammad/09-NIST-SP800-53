# DesertTech Solutions – Risk Assessment Summary

| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Existing Controls | Recommended Controls | Residual Risk |
|------|--------|------------|-------------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Phishing | High | High | Awareness Training | Monthly phishing simulations | Medium |
| Ransomware | Critical | Medium | Antivirus | EDR and offline backups | Low |
| Weak Passwords | High | Medium | Password Policy | Password Manager and MFA | Low |
| Unauthorized Access | Critical | Medium | RBAC | PAM and quarterly reviews | Low |
| Insider Threat | High | Low | HR Screening | User Activity Monitoring | Medium |
| Cloud Misconfiguration | High | Medium | Cloud Policies | CSPM solution | Medium |
| Data Leakage | High | Medium | Access Control | DLP solution | Medium |
| Unpatched Systems | High | High | Monthly Patching | Automated patch management | Low |
| Third-Party Risk | Medium | Medium | Vendor Contracts | Vendor security assessments | Medium |
| Backup Failure | Critical | Low | Daily Backups | Quarterly restore testing | Low |

## Summary

The highest priorities for DesertTech Solutions are strengthening phishing protection, automating patch management, and improving privileged access management.
